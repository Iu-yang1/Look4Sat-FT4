The bundled orekit/UTC-TAI.history is from the Orekit 13.1.4 regular-data reference
set; UTC-TAI relationships originate from IERS. No future leap seconds are assumed.
Update the bundled history when a new leap second becomes effective.

Production propagation uses Orekit's TLE SGP4/SDP4 implementation. Solar and lunar
positions use the bundled 2020-2050 NASA/JPL DE440 subset, generated with JPL's
ASC2EPH converter, or an optional download containing JPL's complete, unmodified
Linux DE440 binary (renamed for Orekit's loader). Geometric occultation uses EclipseDetector/OccultationEngine
and a WGS84 ellipsoid without atmospheric refraction or terrain. IERS Earth
orientation records are used where available; approximations outside their
coverage are reported separately. Numerical tolerances do not guarantee future
orbital prediction accuracy.
